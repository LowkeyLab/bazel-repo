# Book Smartz

Readers build a personal book ranking and preserve their decisions when a save needs checking.

## Language

**Ranking**:
The reader's ordered list of books, from most preferred to least preferred.

**Comparison**:
A question that asks which of two books the reader prefers.

**Choice**:
The book the reader selects as preferred in one comparison.

**Original action**:
A reader's submitted Add, preference, Pause, or Resume, preserved when its outcome needs checking.

**Action evidence**:
Retained facts used to determine whether an original action was saved, rejected, or remains unresolved.
