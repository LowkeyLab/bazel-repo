Mindreadr

A cooperative word-guessing game for two players.

How to Play

  • Both players think of any word and submit it.
  • See both words. Without chatting, each writes a new word that bridges the two.
  • Repeat the process, aiming to converge on the same word.
  • When both submit the exact same word in a round, you win!
