# Prediction Markets

Server members stake points on market outcomes and receive points when markets are resolved.

## Language

**Bettor**:
A member who has placed at least one accepted bet on a particular market.
_Avoid_: Participant when referring specifically to someone who bet on that market

**Position**:
One bettor's accepted stakes in a market, grouped by outcome. Repeated bets on the same outcome contribute to the same position.
_Avoid_: Individual bet, balance

**Payout**:
Points returned to a bettor when a market is resolved, including any returned stake.
_Avoid_: Profit, net gain

**Net result**:
A bettor's payout minus all points they staked in that market. It may be positive, zero, or negative even when they backed the winning outcome.
_Avoid_: Payout, account balance change
